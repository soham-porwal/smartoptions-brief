# Brief methods

The Brief is a deterministic reading of retained observations, not a forecast or an investment recommendation. No model writes its sentences. Missing values are null with a reason; they never become zero. `docs/BRIEF-CONTRACT.md` owns the payload shape.

## Run and verification

From this repository, use its `.venv` if available, otherwise:

```powershell
& D:/soham/myeverything/Code/smartoptions/v2/.venv/Scripts/python.exe -m smartoptions.brief --out app/data
& D:/soham/myeverything/Code/smartoptions/v2/.venv/Scripts/python.exe -m pytest tests/test_brief_company.py tests/test_brief_market.py tests/test_brief_statements.py tests/test_brief_contract.py -q --basetemp logs/bd-tests
```

`--offline` accepts only unexpired cached tables; expired tables become unavailable. `--watchlist AAPL JPM` prioritizes those earnings. No personal watchlist is assumed or read from browser storage. `--tickers AAPL JPM NVDA` is an explicitly partial diagnostic export; omit it for publication. `--now <ISO timestamp>` freezes derived freshness and event windows for reproducibility. Network/cache acquisition timestamps remain actual acquisition times. No prices are claimed to be real-time by this package; quote display and refresh belong to lane BQ.

The runtime verifier checks vocabulary, required fields, provenance, missing reasons, sentence lengths, counts, finite JSON and byte budgets before any payload is written. Every source row receives a company payload or the build fails. Outputs are atomically replaced individually after batch validation. A disk failure can interrupt the multi-file write; rerun to recover. Repeated runs replace the same paths without adding duplicate rows. Fixed inputs plus fixed `--now` produce identical JSON bytes. Cache files are local artifacts, not intended for git or Pages.

## Statements and provenance

Yahoo Finance via yfinance provides quarterly and annual income statements, cash flows, balance sheets, EPS revision counts and earnings estimates. API reference: <https://ranaroussi.github.io/yfinance/reference/api/yfinance.Ticker.html>. Cache keys are `<ticker>.<statement>.json` under `data/cache/brief`, each with its own acquisition timestamp, status and error. TTL is 24 hours, including negative-cache entries. Defaults: four workers, a shared 0.20-second request spacing, two attempts with a one-second backoff. Empty frames count as failed responses and are retried with a fresh provider instance. No key is needed. This does not establish market-data redistribution rights or a service guarantee.

Each emitted Metric has source, URL, reporting period, acquisition/observation date and unit. Statement amounts use Yahoo's estimate-table reporting currency when supplied; otherwise the unit explicitly says `reporting currency`. No FX conversion is performed. Prices are USD/share for the exported US trading universe. Dates on financial metrics are acquisition timestamps; `period` identifies the financial period. Annual statements are retained for audit but do not silently substitute for missing quarterly comparisons.

Quarters must be 60–120 days apart for a four-quarter sum. Year-ago comparisons require a dated quarter 330–400 days earlier. Positive prior revenue/EPS is required for percentage growth; loss-to-profit transitions receive no misleading percentage. Latest statements older than 210 days from the market session make statement-dependent cards unavailable. Yahoo's limited quarterly history often prevents TTM growth: eight contiguous quarters are required, and the package reports this limit rather than treating annual growth as TTM growth. These are current provider snapshots, not point-in-time backtest data; revisions and survivorship affect historical comparisons.

| Dimension | Calculation | Default status rules |
|---|---|---|
| Growth | Latest quarter revenue and diluted EPS / year-ago quarter − 1; revenue TTM = four quarters; TTM growth against previous four quarters | Weak below 0%; strong at least 10%; otherwise ok. Worst available growth component wins. |
| Profitability | Operating income / revenue; change vs year-ago quarter in percentage points | Weak below 5%, strong at least 20%. Direction widening above +0.5pp, narrowing below −0.5pp, otherwise steady. Narrowing caps strong at ok. |
| Cash | Four-quarter operating cash flow minus absolute reported capex; divided by matched TTM net income | Weak if FCF negative or conversion below 0.5x; strong conversion at least 1x; otherwise ok. Nonpositive earnings or unmatched periods make conversion unavailable. |
| Resilience | Total debt / positive equity; net debt / positive TTM EBITDA; current assets / current liabilities; TTM EBIT / absolute TTM interest expense | Weak respectively above 2x, above 4x, below 1x, below 2x. Strong respectively at most 0.5x, at most 1.5x, at least 1.5x, at least 5x. Worst available ratio wins. |
| Valuation | Positive trailing P/E / median positive sector trailing P/E across our exported universe; forward `pef_sec_rel` retained as a separate metric | Strong at most 0.8x, weak above 1.5x, otherwise ok. Cheapness alone does not establish quality. Own history unavailable: `not enough dated EPS history`. |
| Expectations | Yahoo `0q` upLast30days vs downLast30days | More up: strong; more down: weak; equal: ok. Missing counts are unavailable; horizons are never added together. Zero revisions is a valid observation. |
| Counter | Actual weak metrics and earnings within seven calendar days compete by normalized threshold departure | Select maximum severity = 1 + adverse distance / max(abs(weak threshold), 1). Negative FCF uses percentage of TTM revenue, not raw currency. Revision imbalance = 1 + (down−up)/(down+up); near earnings = 1.5. Ties follow dimension order. Only a measured negative is eligible. |

Capex is treated as a cash outflow whether Yahoo represents it as positive or negative; it is not netted against asset-sale proceeds. Net debt uses Yahoo's field, otherwise total debt minus cash equivalents. Net debt/EBITDA requires the balance and income period to match. Interest coverage is unavailable for absent/zero interest expense. Negative book equity does not create a deceptively favorable leverage ratio. Missing ratio components stay visible in the metric flip and do not improve other ratios. Financial/REIT exclusions also apply to counterargument generation.

All thresholds above and scoring weights live in `config/brief.yaml`. Status boundaries: strong is inclusive, weak is strict; equal to the weak boundary is ok. Sector trailing-P/E medians are computed over positive available multiples, not an external benchmark. Other financial thresholds are absolute and tunable; they are **not** claimed to be sector-relative quality scores.

## Sector rules

All `Financials` companies conservatively use TTM net income divided by average current/year-ago equity and assets for profitability (ROE/ROA). Weak below 5%/0.3%, strong at least 15%/1%, respectively; worst available wins. This broad classification also excludes diversified financials from industrial ratio ratings. FCF conversion, current ratio, debt/EBITDA and industrial interest coverage are not rated for financials. Regulatory capital, liquidity coverage, solvency capital and bank-specific credit quality are unavailable because these inputs are not reliably supplied here. REITs and the entire Real Estate sector are conservatively excluded from industrial cash/resilience/profitability and P/E ratings: reliable FFO/AFFO, property debt and suitable peers are not supplied. Growth, revisions and market technicals remain available when measured.

If no measured area is weak, the counter line is exactly `No weak area found in the measured data`. Its status is unavailable when coverage is incomplete, with a dimension-count metric explaining that unmeasured areas cannot be cleared.

## Market assessment

| Assessment | Rule |
|---|---|
| Price trend | Supportive: SPY close > 50-session average > 200-session average and positive 63-session OLS slope. Adverse: reverse order and negative slope. Otherwise mixed, including insufficient history. Slope is OLS price slope / mean price × 100, percent per session. |
| Participation | Percent of matched-session covered names above 50/200-session unadjusted-close averages. Both at least 60%: broadening; both below 40%: narrowing; otherwise mixed. These are level-based descriptors, not a claim about a change in constituents. |
| Growth direction | Latest GDP SAAR growth versus previous quarter and latest three-month payroll average versus preceding three months. Both accelerate above +0.1pp/+5 thousand: up; both decelerate below −0.1pp/−5 thousand: down; otherwise flat (including incomplete evidence). These arrows describe acceleration, not contraction. |
| Inflation direction | Compound last three consecutive CPI/PCE monthly changes and annualize; compare each with its corresponding 12-month YoY. Both gaps >0.25pp: up; both <−0.25pp: down; otherwise flat. Never compare index levels with growth rates. |
| Policy direction | Effective fed funds change over 20 observations: up above +0.1pp, down below −0.1pp, otherwise flat. Curve and next FOMC are exposed in detail, not used as a guessed policy forecast. |
| Backdrop | Supportive: growth up, inflation down, policy not up. Adverse: growth down or inflation/policy both up. Otherwise mixed. |
| Risk | Stressed: VIX ≥30 or HY OAS ≥600bp. Elevated: VIX ≥20, HY ≥450bp, HY 20-observation widening ≥30bp, matched-date VIX/VIX3M >1, a major macro release within seven days, or incomplete core risk evidence. Otherwise calm. |

VIX percentile is the empirical fraction of retained daily values at or below the latest observation, with sample length disclosed. HY change uses 20 available observations, not 20 calendar days. Core macro observations marked stale/error/missing are excluded from assessed values. Each detail group exposes its own main series and dates. Flat arrows can mean mixed or incomplete evidence; the associated line and null metrics distinguish this from confidently unchanged conditions. Monthly and quarterly reporting lags are explicit. The Brief does not infer that an unreleased event was released merely from the current date.

## Positioning and risk calendar

S&P membership is fetched using the existing Wikipedia/constituents CSV adapter. Equities retains every constituent plus existing snapshot-only rows; movers are a view, never a retention filter. Brief pins its membership to the receipt embedded in core, falling back to the 24-hour membership cache for older exports. Only verified members contribute to positioning. The receipt records names, sectors, source URL and symbol mappings. Alphabet GOOG/GOOGL, Fox FOX/FOXA and News Corp NWS/NWSA remain separate securities; BRK.B/BF.B become Yahoo BRK-B/BF-B. No alias invents a return or screener score. A name absent from historical screener has `sam=null`, `sam_status="not in historical screener"`, and the same explicit provenance reason.

Price requests use 50-symbol batches, four download threads, a one-second pause between batches and sequential throttled retries for missing tickers. An incomplete price cache triggers a full requested-universe refresh. Every exported name gets a px file, including an empty-bars file with a missing reason when Yahoo returns no history. `meta.coverage` lists price failures and fatal fundamentals failures; field-level reasons remain in provenance. The current membership receipt and retained-constituent count make round-trip coverage inspectable. No absent ticker is assigned a flat return.

Participation uses matched-session histories separately for 50 and 200 sessions. Its first Metric, headline percentage and sentence all use the 50-session result, rounded once to two decimals. The sentence states the numerator and usable-history denominator, for example `46.12% (232 of 503 names) exceed their 50-session average.` Each Metric note also states unavailable histories; missing histories never count as below-average names. The second Metric retains its own 200-session denominator. UI arrival animations preserve the exact measured figure at every frame. Core `meta.returns_unit` and `row_units` describe percent values in d1/d5/m1; `daily_returns_unit` preserves the separate decimal-fraction convention for equities stage return arrays and relations inputs.

Sector d1/d5/m1 are current-market-cap-weighted percent returns over 1/5/21 sessions from core rows; available-return denominator is used separately per horizon. Sector `mcap` is USD, `n` is covered names, and common period/source metadata is in positioning metrics. Genuine 20-session unadjusted price returns are computed separately from px bars for rotation and top-ten contribution. Leaders/laggards are a descriptive rank, with mixed status rather than a forecast. Top-ten share uses all verified members with available provider market caps, with coverage disclosed. It is a current-provider-cap proxy: Yahoo issuer caps can repeat across share classes, and these are not official free-float S&P weights. The line, dedicated percentage key and first Metric share one two-decimal value. Top-ten contribution sums current-weight × unadjusted 20-session return: an approximation for our covered universe, **not actual S&P index attribution** (no beginning weights, index divisor, corporate-action history or dividend return). Equal vs cap gap uses retained RSP minus SPY 20-session return, and exposes its actual endpoint independently from company aggregates.

Risks cover the next seven calendar days from build time (America/New_York). Macro templates explain possible rate/growth/price effects. Major macro releases have importance 3, EIA 1; watchlist earnings 3, other earnings 2. The landing view shows macro releases first, then watchlist/shortlist earnings, then other earnings; the first six appear initially and the remaining events expand in place. Date-only estimated earnings remain an ISO date with unknown timing; no midnight/BMO/AMC time is invented. Same-day BMO events are excluded after 10 ET. Provider schedules may change; dates are not confirmed-company guidance.

## Shortlist and data health

The shortlist returns up to eight eligible names; it never pads missing coverage to five. Eligibility: at least five available primary dimensions (of six), and measured growth, profitability and valuation. Thus financials/REITs may be excluded by incomplete comparable dimensions. Card points: strong 100, ok 60, weak 20, unavailable 0, averaged over all seven including the counter. Score = 85% card average + 15% technical score, minus 10 for earnings within seven days, clamped 0–100. Technical ordering uses daily price vs 50/200 averages. A favorable retained factor signal adds five technical points (max ten) only when |t|≥2, implied move >0 and its relations-window endpoint is no more than four calendar days before the market session. These historical associations do not predict returns. Reasons are the strongest available dimension sentences; counter is copied with its measured evidence.

Source health includes core stage receipts, oldest individual quote acquisition, each macro group, statement-cache coverage and the historical snapshot. Build freshness and reporting periods are different. Receipt age ≤24h is fresh, >24–48h delayed, >48h stale; an explicit delayed provider remains delayed. Macro group stale/missing flags propagate. Missing quote prices or company tickers absent from the feed mark stale (empty feed missing); incomplete statement coverage is delayed (empty missing). Quote notes separately disclose missing prices among supplied rows and company names entirely absent from the feed. Overall is worst source status, with missing mapped to stale because the overall vocabulary excludes missing. the reference July 25 snapshot stays stale, and the line distinguishes his historic scores from our separately dated observations. The dashboard's overall pill can therefore remain stale even when current quotes refreshed successfully. Source failures are logged under `logs/build-YYYY-MM-DD.jsonl`; provider HTTP/empty-frame details are in the BD run logs. The health popover exposes missing/delayed sources so a log is not the only warning.

## Explore workspace and relationship catalog (2026-10-16 depth pass)

The product design preserves analytical depth on main: the brief stays the two-minute front page, and a restrained **Explore** entry plus drilldowns from brief, company and macro insights open an inspectable comparison workspace. Backup storage keys are untouched; Explore uses its own versioned key `so_brief_explore_v1`.

**Relationship catalog** (`python -m smartoptions.brief.relationships --data-root app/data`, writes `app/data/relationships.json`, schema `brief.relationships.v1`). A fixed candidate list chosen before fitting (63 of a 64 budget): each sector basket vs SPY; each sector basket vs 10-year yield, WTI, DXY and VIX; RSP vs SPY; SPY vs 10-year, DXY, WTI, copper, high-yield OAS, VIX and the 10y-2y spread. Nothing is ranked into the catalog by fit quality. Prices become % returns; yields become basis-point changes; spreads and VIX become changes in their own units. Pairs align on dates both series observed; each change spans consecutive common dates for both series; nothing is forward-filled. Monthly and weekly macro series are excluded from pairing (listed in `skipped_series`) because pairing them with daily changes would create pseudo-samples. Contemporaneous OLS with classic standard errors over the last ≤252 aligned changes (minimum 120), with correlation, 95% slope interval, first/second-half correlations, the SPY-controlled slope for sector-macro pairs, the central 90% of observed factor moves, and the largest-factor-move counterexample against the fitted sign. A chronological 70/30 holdout of the *same-day* fit is scored against a zero-slope baseline: it measures stability, not prediction. Labels: `weak` |t|<2; `stable` |t|≥3, same-sign halves and positive holdout R²; otherwise `unstable`. Invariants (`verify`): schema, budget, n≥120, correlation range, fit window order, sample accounting ≥ n, basket rebasing, and forbidden predictive/causal language. `OK`/`WARN` writes atomically; `FAIL`/`SUSPECT` keeps the previous artifact. Every run logs `brief.relationships` to `logs/build-YYYY-MM-DD.jsonl` and can write a JSON receipt (`--receipt`).

**Sector baskets** are equal-weight averages of current members' daily returns over the SPY session calendar, rebased to 100; a day needs ≥50% member coverage; member returns beyond ±45% are excluded as probable unadjusted splits and counted. They are labelled baskets, never ETFs or official indexes, and carry survivorship (current membership).

**Explore display** (`app/workspace.js`, pure and node-tested): indexed to 100, % change, change in units, standardized (range mean/sample s.d.), raw and natural log; per-series invert (reciprocal in ratio modes, negation otherwise) and a ±20-session visual shift. Rebasing a yield/spread/rate, log/ratio modes with non-positive values, standardizing a constant series, and raw/change/log with mixed units are rejected with the reason and the view falls back to standardized. Transforms never feed statistics. The rebase date is the first date all series share in range. Explore pair statistics recompute aligned-change OLS on the chosen range with sample accounting; non-daily series withhold statistics. An optional statistical lag (≤5 sessions) is descriptive only. The conditional mean is alpha + beta × move. Its Student-t mean band uses residual variance × (1/n + (move − mean X)²/Sxx); the separate individual-day band adds residual variance. Neither is a forecast. Inputs outside the central 90% of observed moves are withheld. Shifted readouts retain original observation dates/values; reverse catalog directions never reuse slopes or bounds.

**Joint economic scenarios:** the total fixed search remains64 candidates:46 pair candidates and18 joint rate/oil models for11 sectors and SPY/AAPL/MSFT/NVDA/AMZN/JPM/XOM. Each uses at most252 common dated changes, ≥120 samples, an intercept, Treasury changes in bp and WTI returns in percent. Standardized design condition number>30, singular/constant predictors, out-of-range inputs and joint leverage>9/n withhold estimates. Mean uncertainty uses residual variance × v′(X′X)⁻¹v, including covariance cross terms; individual-day variation adds residual variance. A distinct what-if marker and shaded mean band use the last observed chart price as a graphical anchor; they never add a historical or future bar. Reset removes only the hypothetical overlay. Unsupported company models retain historical comparisons and explain the fixed budget.

**Map and refresh integrity:** map area uses snapshot market capitalization; signed color is covered cap-weighted return within one stated regular session, never an all-live index claim. Sector chart baskets remain equally weighted and are clearly distinguished. Missing inputs are grey/countable; smaller sectors have keyboard targets and an accessible touch list. Quote-only updates preserve controls and chart identity. A quote predating the newly refreshed history cannot drive current valuation or interpretation. Optional statement/macro outages retain prior valid observations with actual source dates, explicit reasons and degraded health; historical verified release dates retain their verification dates while event studies are recomputed. CTVA audit provenance is factored into reference tables to preserve every guarded observation within the12KB company limit. Safe failure receipts identify stage/code/ticker/byte or factor counts. Required relationship generation, verification, bundle hashes and mirror gates run in the existing coordinated refresh.

**Live quotes in Explore** come from the single `SmartOptionsLive` engine (owner `explore`, ranked after `company` inside the 100-name stream bound; no extra socket or poller). Only a usable regular-session quote (provider flag 1, or no flag and a 9:30-16:15 ET weekday trade) touches the daily line: same session updates the last close, a later session appends a provisional point. Older, future-stamped, extended-session, stale/blocked and missing quotes change nothing; extended trades are reported separately. Relative performance ends on the last date both series have, so a benchmark without a same-session price is never compared. Macro readings and fitted coefficients keep their dates and do not tick.

## Daily changes, participation wording and event horizon (r13)
- **SPY previous session:** last two retained SPY closes; fails closed (unavailable) when the latest close is not `asof_session`. Both dates are published.
- **Participation five-session change:** one fixed cohort on the shared SPY session index: names with bars on both the session and the fifth prior session and at least 50 closes up to each date. The share above the 50-session average is computed for the same names on both dates; excluded names are counted. Closes are unadjusted, so a split inside the window can create a false crossing. Fewer than 90% of members in the cohort → unavailable.
- **Level words:** `broad` when both 50- and 200-session shares are ≥60%, `thin` when both are <40%, otherwise `mixed` — the same thresholds as the older `broadening/narrowing` status, which classified levels and is retained only for compatibility.
- **Equal minus cap weight:** the RSP−SPY 20-session gap today versus five sessions earlier, from the same retained history; the latest value must equal the history's last point and be dated `asof_session`.
- **Sector moves:** one-session cap-weighted returns on matched closes, weighted by current market values (declared).
- **Macro latest vs prior:** the last two observations of the retained history with their own observation dates; the history must end on the observation's date. Values come from the current data vintage and may include revisions; a newer retrieval without a new observation is not a change.
- **Event horizon:** ET calendar dates from today through today+N for both macro releases and earnings; timed releases earlier today are dropped. A same-day before-open report is dropped from 10:00 ET (threshold choice). Provider timing other than BMO/AMC is treated as date-only. Coverage is partial: no provider supplies a range receipt, so the latest listed release is never treated as the end of coverage.
- **Risk conditions:** observed volatility and credit only. Scheduled major releases are reported as `macro.risk_events` and no longer elevate the observed state.
# Private daily review (investor pass, 2026-10-18)

Saved companies are a list of followed names, never an equal-weight portfolio. New position entries are limited to current covered S&P common equities; retained former/non-index entries remain visible with unsupported coverage rather than being assumed to be common equity. Positions are optional entered long USD equity quantities, not a complete account, net worth or broker reconciliation. Main and the expanded import UI share `local-data.js` and the existing `so_import_v1` book. Legacy `so_local_imports_v1` is read without migration or mutation. Metrics, series, unrelated holdings and unknown fields survive additive book merges. Every conflicting position replacement requires an explicit choice. Duplicate lots use summed quantities and quantity-weighted confirmed per-share costs; total costs are summed before division. A missing lot quantity blocks the ticker's quantity; a missing lot cost withholds its aggregate cost. Ambiguous cost headers require a unit choice. Non-USD input is unsupported.

Value is entered quantity times the canonical usable USD price. Confirmed per-share cost enables unrealized amount = quantity × (price − cost). Legacy cost alone is not certified. Complete entered-equity coverage permits the entered equity value label; partial coverage is a priced subtotal with priced/entered counts and share-of-priced-subtotal weights. Missing values prevent a value-based coverage percentage. Unsupported, missing and zero/inactive positions remain distinct. The canonical quote engine governs usable observations; source times remain visible.

Session contribution is quantity × (accepted price − dated prior regular close), conditional on an explicit previous-regular-close basis, a verified prior date, matching return/observation session, and a regular observation. A whole-book total also requires complete comparable current/prior sessions. Current Finnhub snapshots without a previous-close date cannot support these dollars; percentage moves remain dated. Quantities are assumed held over the interval, excluding trades, dividends and fees. These are not account returns.

Review items merge reasons per ticker: coverage preventing interpretation first; changed earnings information or due personal review date; earnings inside the selected 7/30-day horizon; then absolute latest-session move. Saved names rank by percentage. Positions use dollars only when all active entries have comparable dated bases, otherwise a labeled percentage fallback. Ties use ticker order. At most three items appear initially, with pages of up to 24. Calendar coverage remains incomplete/unknown as documented by the shared event calendar.

The separate review clock changes only on Mark reviewed. `so_investor_v1` version 1 stores notes, optional review dates and per-scope checkpoints. The checkpoint retains a scope/name/quantity fingerprint and actually available completed session, next earnings date/timing/source and usable-price coverage, with quote times for display. It does not establish performance since a visit, fundamental revisions, thesis stability or a full research history. New/edited entries, unavailable comparisons, newer sessions and unchanged tracked fields have distinct labels.

Driver detail uses dated `att` only when actual = explained + unexplained and the factor breakdown reconciles within rounding tolerance. A different quote session makes it prior-session analysis. The remainder is unexplained, not proven company news or alpha. Factor relationships require at least 120 common sessions; units, t statistics, fit window, R² and available 60-session coefficients remain visible. Opposite short/long signs are flagged. |t| ≥2 is an unadjusted screen, not a probability. Macro event channels are possible economic links, not known impact signs. Company what-ifs retain the existing bounds, leverage/condition checks and distinct mean/individual-day intervals; no portfolio intervals are added.

Private state has no network client, share or export path. Personal questions are fenced before context loading and remote planning, and marked `noPolish`; rephrasing itself also enforces that flag. Public company chat retains public facts. Storage saves compare original raw bytes, verify readback and retain an in-memory copy on blocked/full storage with a visible warning. Storage events announce cross-tab changes; explicit reload/re-preview is required before a conflicting save. Preview and client origins do not share local data, and there is no implied cloud/phone synchronization.
